## Accuracy and the ingredient scanner

We work to keep information accurate and current, and we list sources on our medical guides (see our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/)). But research changes, product formulas change without notice, and people with AGS react differently. The ingredient scanner flags ingredient names that *may* come from mammals; it cannot tell you whether a specific product is safe for you, and it can be wrong or incomplete, especially with unclear photos or partial ingredient lists. Always read the current label and check with the manufacturer, your pharmacist, or your allergist when it matters.

Recipes are checked for common alpha-gal triggers, but you are responsible for checking the labels of the ingredients you buy and for deciding what is safe for you.
