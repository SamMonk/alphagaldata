Reese pieces cookies with peanut buttercream icing

2 1/4 cups all purpose flour
2 teaspoons cornstarch
1 teaspoon baking powder
1/2 teaspoon baking soda
1 1/2 teaspoons salt
3/4 cup country crock plant butter at room temperature (I prefer salted)
3/4 cup brown sugar
1/2 cup granulated sugar
1 large egg at room temperature
2 teaspoons vanilla extract (I use Nielsen-Massey paste)
1/2 cup Reese’s Pieces (make sure it’s the dairy free formulation )
1/2  Guittard Dairy Free Chocolate Chips
1/4 cup Reese’s Peanut Butter Chips

Preheat oven to 375
Line baking sheet with parchment or use a silicone baking mat

 Combine your dry ingredients (minus chips) and set aside

Cream butter and sugars on medium speed for 2-3 minutes, scraping the sides as needed until smooth and combined.

Add the egg and vanilla, mix another minute.

Add your dry mix until just combined. 

Roll out 2” balls and place on cookie sheet. Bake for 8-10 minutes, depending on your oven. Should be light golden brown when done.

To make the frosting, combine

1/2 cup country crock plant butter at room temperature
1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
1 1/2 cups confection sugar
1/2 teaspoon salt
1 teaspoon vanilla 
2 tablespoons oat milk (as needed)

Combine peanut butter and softened butter in mixer until smooth. 

Slowly add confection sugar in 1/2 cup increments and combine until smooth. 

Add vanilla and salt, then add milk in small increments to reach desired consistency.

Apply frosting how you see fit. Works great as a sandwich cookie filling.
