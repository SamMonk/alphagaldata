ingredients:
  - "1 tablespoon olive oil"
  - "1 ½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces (plain or label-verified prepared chicken)"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "1 large yellow onion, diced"
  - "4 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "5 cups chicken broth (carrageenan-free)"
  - "2 cups chopped, roasted green chiles (or frozen diced chiles)"
  - "1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes with juice"
  - "1 ½ cups tomatillo salsa"
  - "½ teaspoon dried oregano"
  - "1 pinch ground cloves"
steps:
  - "Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven or large pot over medium-high heat. Season chicken with salt and pepper, then cook in the hot oil until browned on all sides, about 7 minutes. The chicken will finish cooking in the chili; use a food thermometer later to verify 165°F in the thickest pieces. Transfer chicken to a plate."
  - "Reduce heat to medium. Add onion and garlic to the pot. Cook, stirring, until onion has softened and turned translucent, about 5 minutes."
  - "Return chicken to the pot. Stir in chicken broth, green chiles, diced tomatoes with juice, and tomatillo salsa. Season with oregano and ground cloves. Bring to a simmer over medium-high heat."
  - "Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes."
  - "Transfer 2 cups of the liquid (without chicken pieces) to a blender. Hold the lid firmly with a folded kitchen towel. Pulse a few times before blending until smooth. Pour back into the pot — this thickens the chili and smooths out the chile chunks."
  - "Continue to simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, until chicken is very tender and flavors are melded, at least 35 more minutes. Adjust salt and pepper to taste. Serve over baked burritos or with beans and rice."
