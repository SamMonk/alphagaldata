Tinfoil trout with garlic butter

One piece of trout approx 1.5lbs, with the skin on
Juice from 1 lemon
4-5 cloves of garlic, smashed and diced fine
1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
3 tbsp country crock plant butter
Salt and pepper to taste

Preheat oven to 375
Lightly coat a piece of tinfoil with olive oil (you want enough foil to create a tent for the fish to cook it fully enclosed in the foil)
Put trout on foil, skin side down. 
Apply lemon juice to entire piece of trout
Sprinkle with salt and pepper
Put plant butter in a bowl and melt it in microwave (30-45 seconds)
Pour chopped garlic in melted butter
Spoon garlic butter all over trout evenly and sprinkle parsley over trout

Seal up tinfoil and cook for 17-20 mins at 375.
Open up foil, turn oven to Hi broil and cook for an additional 2 minutes, paying close attention to prevent burning.

The skin will separate easily with a spatula. Serve with steamed veggies and rice or potatoes.
