safety_notes:
  - "Avocados, tomato, cilantro, onion, lime, garlic salt, cumin, and chili powder are all simple ingredients, but still re-check the labels on any packaged spices or seasoned salt you use."
  - "For the brightest flavor, add the lime juice as you mix so you can taste and adjust before serving."
  - "If you store leftovers, refrigerate them promptly and press plastic wrap directly against the surface before sealing the container to slow oxidation."
substitutions:
  - "Use lemon juice if you are out of limes, then adjust the amount to taste."
  - "Use less onion or cilantro if you prefer a milder guacamole."
  - "Leave the chili powder out if you want a simpler, more classic avocado dip."
caution_points:
  - "Do not overmix if you want the avocados to stay a little chunky."
  - "The garlic salt and chili powder are approximate on purpose, so season gradually and stop when it tastes right to you."
ingredients:
  - "4-5 avocados"
  - "1 Roma or Italian tomato, chopped"
  - "1 head cilantro, chopped"
  - "1 onion, chopped"
  - "1-2 limes"
  - "1-2 teaspoons garlic salt"
  - "1 teaspoon cumin"
  - "1/2 teaspoon chili powder"
steps:
  - "Cut the avocados and place them in a mixing bowl."
  - "Add half of the chopped tomato, cilantro, and onion, along with half of the garlic salt, cumin, and chili powder."
  - "Juice one lime over the bowl and mix until combined."
  - "Add the remaining avocado and the rest of the vegetables and spices."
  - "Mix again until everything is well combined, but leave some avocado chunks if you like a chunkier texture."
  - "Taste and add more lime juice or seasoning if needed."
  - "To store, refrigerate promptly, press a layer of plastic wrap directly against the guacamole, then seal it in a container to help prevent oxidation."
