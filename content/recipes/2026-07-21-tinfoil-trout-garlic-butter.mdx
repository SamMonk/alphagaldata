safety_summary: "Trout is fish, not mammalian meat, and swapping dairy butter for plant butter keeps this dish free of the mammal-derived ingredients most alpha-gal diets avoid, as long as the plant butter label is verified."
safety_notes:
  - "Trout is a fish rather than a mammalian meat, but the finished dish still depends on the exact butter, marinade, sauce, and cross-contact conditions."
  - "Country Crock Plant Butter: verify the tub or stick you buy is plant-based and does not list dairy-derived or other mammal-derived ingredients, since some spreads blend in dairy."
  - "Buy trout that is just fish with no added marinades, basting butters, or sauces that could introduce dairy or other mammal-derived ingredients."
  - "When the plant butter and all other ingredients are verified for your needs, this recipe is intended to avoid mammalian meat, lard, gelatin, and dairy. Re-check labels with each purchase."
substitutions:
  - "Use any dairy-free plant butter or a neutral oil such as olive or avocado oil in place of Country Crock, but confirm the label first."
  - "Salmon or another skin-on fish fillet works with the same method and timing adjusted for thickness."
  - "Use dried parsley if you do not have fresh; start with about a teaspoon and adjust to taste."
caution_points:
  - "If you are also dairy-sensitive, do not assume every 'plant butter' is dairy-free — read the ingredient list on the exact package in hand."
  - "Cook fish to 145°F in the thickest portion as measured with a food thermometer, then watch the final broil closely because thin fillets can burn quickly."
ingredients:
  - "1 trout fillet, about 1.5 lbs, skin on"
  - "Juice of 1 lemon"
  - "4-5 cloves garlic, smashed and finely diced"
  - "1 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped"
  - "3 tablespoons Country Crock Plant Butter (or other verified dairy-free plant butter)"
  - "Salt and pepper, to taste"
  - "Olive oil, for coating the foil"
steps:
  - "Preheat the oven to 375 F."
  - "Lightly coat a large piece of foil with olive oil. Use enough foil to fold into a sealed tent so the fish can cook fully enclosed."
  - "Place the trout on the foil, skin side down."
  - "Squeeze the lemon juice over the entire fillet, then sprinkle with salt and pepper."
  - "Put the plant butter in a small bowl and melt it in the microwave, about 30 to 45 seconds."
  - "Stir the diced garlic into the melted butter."
  - "Spoon the garlic butter evenly over the trout, then sprinkle the chopped parsley on top."
  - "Fold and seal the foil into a closed tent and bake for 17 to 20 minutes at 375°F. Check the thickest portion with a food thermometer and continue cooking until it reaches 145°F."
  - "Open the foil, switch the oven to high broil, and cook 2 more minutes, watching closely to prevent burning."
  - "The skin will separate easily with a spatula. Serve with steamed vegetables and rice or potatoes."
