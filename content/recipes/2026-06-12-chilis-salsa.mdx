ingredients:
  - "4 teaspoons canned diced jalapeño chiles"
  - "1/4 cup diced yellow onion"
  - "1 (14.5-ounce) can diced tomatoes with green chiles"
  - "1 (14.5-ounce) can diced tomatoes with juice"
  - "1/2 to 3/4 teaspoon garlic salt"
  - "1/2 to 3/4 teaspoon ground cumin"
  - "1/4 teaspoon sugar"
steps:
  - "Place the jalapeños and onion in a food processor."
  - "Process for just a few seconds."
  - "Add both cans of tomatoes, the garlic salt, cumin, and sugar."
  - "Process until everything is well blended, but do not puree."
  - "Transfer the salsa to a container, cover it, and chill well before serving."
