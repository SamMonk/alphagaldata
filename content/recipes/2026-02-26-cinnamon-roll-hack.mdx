ingredients:
  - "1 (8 oz) package Pillsbury Crescent Roll Sheets (verify the exact package)"
  - "2–3 tablespoons label-verified dairy-free plant butter, softened"
  - "¼ cup brown sugar"
  - "1 teaspoon cinnamon (adjust to taste)"
  - "1 cup powdered sugar (for frosting)"
  - "2 tablespoons plant butter, melted (for frosting)"
  - "Label-verified oat milk as needed for frosting consistency"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper."
  - "Mix brown sugar and cinnamon together in a small bowl. Set aside."
  - "Unroll the crescent sheet on a clean surface. Spread softened plant butter liberally over the entire surface."
  - "Sprinkle the cinnamon-sugar mixture evenly over the buttered sheet."
  - "Roll the sheet up tightly from one long edge. Cut into 8 equal portions and place cut-side up on the prepared baking sheet."
  - "Bake 10–12 minutes until golden brown."
  - "While rolls bake, make the frosting: whisk together powdered sugar and melted plant butter. Add oat milk 1 tablespoon at a time, stirring until smooth but not too thin."
  - "Allow rolls to cool at least 5 minutes before frosting. Drizzle or spread frosting over warm rolls and enjoy."
