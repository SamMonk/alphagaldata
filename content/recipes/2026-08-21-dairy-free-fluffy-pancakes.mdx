ingredients:
  - "2 cups all-purpose flour"
  - "1/4 cup granulated sugar"
  - "4 teaspoons baking powder"
  - "1/4 teaspoon baking soda"
  - "1/2 teaspoon salt"
  - "1 3/4 cups label-verified oat milk"
  - "1/4 cup label-verified dairy-free plant butter, melted and slightly cooled"
  - "2 teaspoons vanilla extract"
  - "1 large egg"
  - "Plant butter or neutral oil, for greasing the pan"
steps:
  - "Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl."
  - "In a separate bowl, whisk together the oat milk, slightly cooled melted plant butter, vanilla, and egg."
  - "Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and fold just until combined. A few small lumps are fine. Let the batter rest while the pan heats."
  - "Heat a nonstick skillet or griddle over low to medium heat and lightly grease it with verified plant butter or oil."
  - "Pour about 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake. Cook until the underside is golden and bubbles appear across the surface."
  - "Flip once and cook until golden and set in the center. Repeat with the remaining batter, lightly greasing the pan as needed."
  - "Serve warm with fruit, syrup, or another label-verified topping."
