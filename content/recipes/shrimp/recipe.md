Shrimp pasta with garlic and lemon

2/3 box linguini, cooked all dente
1 lb shrimp (we use 31-40 size, frozen, tails off)
4 cloves of garlic, finely chopped
1/2-3/4 tsp red pepper flakes
4 tbsp plant butter, divided
1 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp Italian seasoning (our favorite is Sam’s Club Italian spice grinder)
1 tbsp fresh chopped parsley
(Optional: 2 cups spinach)
Juice from 1 lemon


Chop your garlic and parsley, then set aside

Start your pasta according to the instructions for al dente
Drain and set aside
Season your defrosted shrimp with Italian seasoning
In the same pot, add oil and 2tbsp plant butter to melt down
Add your chopped garlic and red pepper and cook on medium until fragrant…no more than a minute or so
Add your shrimp and cook until just pink, about 2 mins. Flip and cook one more minute on other side. 
Once pink, add your drained pasta and remaining butter to the pot and stir. If you opted for spinach, add it now and let it wilt down. Once incorporated, remove from heat and add lemon juice to the dish. 

If we have Rebel Parmesan Cheese, we add it to the dish as we plate it. If you have a non dairy Parmesan cheese you prefer, add it to the dish!
