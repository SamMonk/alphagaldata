safety_notes:
  - "Choose plain shrimp or verify any prepared shrimp package for marinades and added ingredients. Thaw frozen shrimp safely in the refrigerator or according to the package directions."

substitutions:

caution_points:
  - "Cook the shrimp until opaque and firm and verify the thickest piece reaches 145°F with a food thermometer."
  - "Avoid overcooking the shrimp; it can become tough quickly after it is fully cooked."
ingredients:
  - "8 ounces linguini"
  - "1 pound shrimp, 31-40 size, thawed, peeled, and tails removed"
  - "4 cloves garlic, finely chopped"
  - "1/2 to 3/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes"
  - "4 tablespoons label-verified dairy-free plant butter, divided"
  - "1 tablespoon olive oil"
  - "1 tablespoon label-verified Italian seasoning"
  - "1 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped"
  - "2 cups spinach, optional"
  - "Juice of 1 lemon"
  - "Label-verified non-dairy Parmesan, optional"
steps:
  - "Cook the linguini in salted water until al dente according to the package directions. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, then drain."
  - "Pat the thawed shrimp dry and season it with the Italian seasoning."
  - "Return the pot to medium heat. Add the olive oil and 2 tablespoons of the plant butter."
  - "Add the garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook for no more than 1 minute, just until fragrant."
  - "Add the shrimp in a single layer. Cook until opaque and firm, about 2 minutes on the first side and 1 to 2 minutes on the other side. Verify the thickest shrimp reaches 145°F."
  - "Add the drained pasta and remaining plant butter. Toss until the butter melts, adding a splash of reserved pasta water if needed."
  - "If using spinach, add it and toss until wilted. Remove the pot from the heat and add the lemon juice and parsley."
  - "Serve immediately with optional label-verified non-dairy Parmesan."
