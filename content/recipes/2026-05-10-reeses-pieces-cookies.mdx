safety_notes:
  - "Country Crock Plant Butter: verify the tub or stick you buy is plant-based and does not list dairy-derived ingredients."
  - "Reese's Pieces: verify the exact package for ingredients and shared-line disclosures. A product that works for one person or package may not work for another."
  - "Chocolate chips: Guittard dairy-free chocolate chips are the intended option here; if substituting, confirm the replacement is dairy-free."
  - "Peanut butter chips: many peanut butter chips contain milk ingredients. Use only a verified dairy-free option, or omit them and increase the Reese's Pieces or dairy-free chocolate chips."
  - "Oat milk: choose a carrageenan-free oat milk if carrageenan is a personal trigger."
  - "This recipe avoids mammalian meat, lard, gelatin, and dairy when the packaged ingredients are verified as listed. Re-check labels with each purchase."
substitutions:
  - "Omit the peanut butter chips if you cannot find a label-verified dairy-free option."
  - "Use extra dairy-free chocolate chips or Reese's Pieces in place of uncertain peanut butter chips."
  - "Use another dairy-free plant butter if Country Crock is unavailable, but confirm the label before baking."
caution_points:
  - "Do not assume Reese's Pieces or peanut butter chips are safe across stores or dates; verify the exact package in hand."
  - "The frosting depends on plant butter and oat milk, so re-check both labels for dairy and carrageenan before mixing."
ingredients:
  - "2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour"
  - "2 teaspoons cornstarch"
  - "1 teaspoon baking powder"
  - "1/2 teaspoon baking soda"
  - "1 1/2 teaspoons salt"
  - "3/4 cup Country Crock Plant Butter, room temperature"
  - "3/4 cup brown sugar"
  - "1/2 cup granulated sugar"
  - "1 large egg, room temperature"
  - "2 teaspoons vanilla extract or vanilla bean paste"
  - "1/2 cup Reese's Pieces, from a label-verified package"
  - "1/2 cup Guittard dairy-free chocolate chips"
  - "1/4 cup dairy-free peanut butter chips, or omit if unavailable"
  - "1/2 cup Country Crock Plant Butter, room temperature, for frosting"
  - "1/2 cup creamy peanut butter"
  - "1 1/2 cups confectioners' sugar"
  - "1/2 teaspoon salt, for frosting"
  - "1 teaspoon vanilla, for frosting"
  - "2 tablespoons oat milk, or as needed for frosting consistency"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 375 F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat."
  - "Whisk together the flour, cornstarch, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl. Set aside."
  - "Cream the plant butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar on medium speed for 2-3 minutes, scraping down the sides as needed, until smooth and combined."
  - "Add the egg and vanilla. Mix for about 1 minute."
  - "Add the dry ingredients and mix just until combined."
  - "Fold in the Reese's Pieces, dairy-free chocolate chips, and verified dairy-free peanut butter chips."
  - "Roll the dough into 2-inch balls and place them on the prepared baking sheet."
  - "Bake for 8-10 minutes, depending on your oven, until the cookies are light golden brown. Cool before frosting."
  - "To make the frosting, beat the peanut butter and softened plant butter until smooth."
  - "Slowly add confectioners' sugar in 1/2-cup increments, mixing until smooth after each addition."
  - "Add vanilla and salt, then add oat milk in small increments until the frosting reaches your desired consistency."
  - "Spread or pipe the frosting onto cooled cookies, or use it as a sandwich cookie filling."
